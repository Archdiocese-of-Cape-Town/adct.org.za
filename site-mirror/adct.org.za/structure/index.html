Home
  • Cape Town is one of South Africa's archdioceses and serves as a metropolitan see.
  • Recovered summaries name the suffragan dioceses as Aliwal, De Aar, Oudtshoorn, Port Elizabeth, and Queenstown.
  • The archbishop is assisted by vicars general, a chancellor, department heads, a presbyteral council, deans, and pastoral structures.
  • The archdiocese is described as comprising 80 parishes with pastoral and finance councils, coordinated through a wider Archdiocesan Pastoral Council.
