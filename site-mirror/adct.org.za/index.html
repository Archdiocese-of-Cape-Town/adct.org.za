Welcome

The site presents the Catholic Church in Cape Town as the oldest ecclesiastical territory in Southern Africa, established in 1818 in South Africa's “Mother City”. Publicly indexed text describes the website as a place to learn about the Church's life, ministries, parishes, resources, and service to the wider community.

This mirror preserves recoverable public information only. It does not include downloadable assets from the original domain because external DNS resolution was unavailable during this task.
