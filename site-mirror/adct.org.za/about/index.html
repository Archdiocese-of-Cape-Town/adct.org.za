Publicly visible descriptions identify the Archdiocese of Cape Town as the oldest ecclesiastical territory in Southern Africa, established in 1818 and centred on Cape Town.

The archdiocese's mother church is the Cathedral of St. Mary of the Flight into Egypt. Indexed descriptions present the site as an introduction to the archdiocese's history, mission, leadership, ministries, and pastoral service.

Recovered search summaries also describe the present Archbishop as Most Reverend Sithembele Sipuka, with a pastoral emphasis on being “United and Sent”.
